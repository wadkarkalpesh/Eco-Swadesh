Architecture &amp; Core Systems 2.1 Multi-Persona Architecture The application features dynamic state-driven switching across 6 target user personas: 🌾 Farmer Persona: Focused on bio-input procurement, soil dosage calculation, organic subsidy discovery, and AI crop disease diagnosis. 🛒 Retail Consumer Persona: Enables buying fresh organic produce and home garden fertilizers in small retail packages (Kg/Liters). 🚛 Bulk Commercial Buyer Persona: Designed for grain mills, wholesalers, and exporters to purchase harvest truckloads in Tons with escrow protection. 🧪 Fertilizer Seller Persona: Allows bio-input manufacturers and local cooperatives to manage listings, upload lab purity certificates, and track sales. 👨‍🔬 Agri Expert Persona: Provides verified credentials to answer soil queries, publish organic guides, and accept paid consultations. 🛡️ Platform Admin Persona: Manages platform trust, seller license verification, organic certification compliance, and dispute resolution. 2.2 Global Localization &amp; Multi-Currency Built with an extensible internationalization (i18n) translation engine and multi-currency formatter. Users can seamlessly toggle between 7 global languages: English, Hindi (हिंदी), Spanish (Español), French (Français), Swahili (Kiswahili), Arabic (العربية), and Bahasa Indonesia. Supported currencies include INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), and AUD (A$), with real-time conversion across both Retail (per Kg/Liter) and Bulk (per Ton) pricing tiers. 3.
